How to Dry Clothes with a Dehumidifier

A simple room setup can make more difference than running the appliance for longer.

Updated 24 September 2026·Independent research guide
Quick answer

Put the airer in a small heated room, space garments so air can move, close the external windows and doors, place the dehumidifier beside the airer with clear airflow, then use laundry mode.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Spin the load well.A faster final spin leaves less water for the room and dehumidifier to remove.
  2. Use a manageable room.A smaller closed room is usually faster than trying to treat the whole home.
  3. Space the clothes.Avoid folded or overlapping fabric; leave gaps for air movement.
  4. Position the appliance safely.Keep the inlet and outlet clear and follow the manufacturer’s minimum clearances.
  5. Select laundry mode.On the Arete One, the dedicated mode is designed around a six-hour cycle.
  6. Rotate heavy items halfway.Turn thick waistbands, towels or hoodies so damp areas face the airflow.

Should windows be open?

During active dehumidification, open windows usually let more outside air enter and make the appliance work on a moving target. Close external windows in the chosen room while the machine runs. Continue normal safe ventilation at other times and address persistent damp at its source.

How long should it take?

Meaco describes its smart laundry cycle as six hours. Actual drying time depends on room temperature, load size, fabric, final spin speed and spacing. Treat six hours as a useful cycle rather than a guarantee that every heavy garment will be cupboard-dry.

Common mistakes

  • Placing wet garments directly over the appliance
  • Overloading a small airer so fabric touches
  • Using a compressor dehumidifier in a very cold unheated room
  • Leaving the room open to the whole house
  • Ignoring leaks, mould or structural damp
