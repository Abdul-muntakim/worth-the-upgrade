Sage Barista Express Cleaning and Descaling Guide

Cleaning coffee oils and removing mineral scale are different jobs—your machine needs both at the right time.

Updated 24 September 2026·Independent research guide
Quick answer

Wipe and purge the steam wand after every use, rinse the portafilter and basket daily, clean the grinder area regularly, run the cleaning cycle when prompted, and descale according to water hardness and the official instructions.

Cleaning is not descaling

The cleaning cycle targets coffee oils in the brew path using the cleaning disc and a suitable tablet. Descaling targets mineral deposits from water. Do not treat the CLEAN ME reminder as permission to pour descaler through an unrelated cycle.

A practical maintenance schedule

After every milk drinkWipe and purge the steam wand
DailyRinse basket and portafilter; wipe group area
WeeklyClean drip tray, water tank and accessible grinder area
When promptedRun the official cleaning cycle
By water condition/manualDescale using the model instructions

How to run the cleaning cycle safely

Use the correct single-wall basket, cleaning disc and approved tablet arrangement for your BES875 revision, then follow the button sequence in the manual or Sage tutorial. Keep the portafilter locked in place until the programme finishes.

Steam-wand blockage prevention

Purge immediately after steaming and wipe while residue is still soft. If holes become blocked, use the supplied cleaning tool only when the wand is cool. Never leave the tip soaking in milk.

Protect flavour as well as the machine

  • Use fresh water and replace the water filter on schedule
  • Discard stale grounds around the grinder outlet
  • Backflush when prompted rather than waiting for poor flow
  • Follow the manual for your exact model number
