Blueair Blue Max 3250i Filter Replacement Guide

Good airflow and the correct replacement filter matter more than a calendar guess.

Updated 24 September 2026·Independent research guide
Quick answer

Vacuum or wash the outer fabric pre-filter as directed, but replace rather than wash the internal particle-and-carbon filter. Use the filter-life indicator and actual operating conditions as your guide.

Know the two filter layers

The coloured fabric outside is a reusable pre-filter that catches larger dust and hair. The internal particle-and-carbon filter is the working replacement component. Cleaning the fabric can restore airflow, but it does not renew the inner filter.

When should the filter be replaced?

Use the purifier’s filter indicator and app information where available. A home with pets, smoke, renovation dust or long daily runtime can load a filter faster than a lightly used bedroom. Reduced airflow or persistent odour can also justify checking it sooner.

Replacement checklist

  1. Switch off and unplug.Move the purifier to a clear floor area.
  2. Remove the outer pre-filter.Clean it separately according to Blueair’s care instructions.
  3. Open the filter compartment.Note the old filter’s orientation before lifting it out.
  4. Fit the correct 3250i filter.Remove all packaging and avoid crushing the media.
  5. Reassemble and reset.Follow the model instructions to reset the filter indicator.

How to make a filter last sensibly

  • Keep the washable pre-filter clean
  • Do not block air intake with curtains or furniture
  • Vacuum rooms normally so the purifier is not the only dust-control method
  • Avoid running the unit beside an open window when outdoor pollution is high

Budget before buying

The replacement filter is the main recurring ownership cost. Check current UK availability and price before choosing the purifier, especially if you intend to run it continuously.
