Editorial standards

How we research
every recommendation.

Clear separation between verified facts, research judgements and genuine hands-on experience.

Our promise

We do not claim to have personally tested a product unless the page explicitly describes that testing. Current reviews are research-based and labelled that way.

1. Start with the buyer’s problem

We define the practical job first: supporting long desk sessions, controlling humidity, filtering particles or simplifying a daily routine. That prevents a long feature list from becoming the recommendation by default.

2. Check primary information

Manufacturer product pages, manuals and support documents are used for specifications and operating guidance. We then compare those facts with recurring buyer concerns and reputable independent coverage where it adds context.

3. Explain who should not buy

A useful review needs drawbacks, fit limits and alternatives. We avoid invented scores and do not present illustrative images as exact product photography.

4. Re-check dated facts

Each guide carries an update date. Retailer variants, warranties, prices, filters and specifications can change, so readers are directed to verify the exact live listing before purchasing.

5. Keep commission separate

Some retailer links are affiliate links. A qualifying purchase may earn the site a commission without increasing the buyer’s price. Affiliate status does not decide the order of recommendations.

Corrections

If manufacturer information changes, we update the relevant page and date. Material uncertainty is stated directly rather than filled with an assumption.
