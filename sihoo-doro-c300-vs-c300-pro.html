SIHOO Doro C300 vs C300 Pro: Which Chair Fits You?

The Pro adds adjustment range; the standard C300 keeps the idea simpler and usually costs less.

Updated 24 September 2026·Independent research guide
Quick answer

Choose the standard C300 if you want adaptive support at the lower price. Consider the C300 Pro if seat-depth adjustment and a wider range of armrest or recline control matter to your fit.

The practical difference

Both chairs use SIHOO’s dynamic-support approach, with mesh contact surfaces and lumbar support intended to respond as posture changes. The C300 Pro is the more adjustable version. That matters most for people who struggle to match a fixed seat depth or want finer control over the armrests and recline.

Choose the C300 when…

  • You are within the stated fit range and do not need adjustable seat depth
  • You want breathable mesh and dynamic lumbar support at the more accessible price
  • You prefer fewer controls to set up
  • The current retailer offers the better return policy

Choose the C300 Pro when…

  • You often find chair seats too deep or too shallow
  • You share the chair with someone of a different build
  • You want more control over arm position
  • You will use the chair for long sessions and fit matters more than saving the price difference

Measure before deciding

Sit with your back against the backrest and check that a small gap remains behind the knees. Confirm the lowest seat setting lets your feet rest securely, then measure whether the armrests can pass under your desk. A longer feature list cannot rescue a chair that is the wrong size.
